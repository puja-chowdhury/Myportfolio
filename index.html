01Course design & delivery

Salesforce Administrator Learning Journey

An end-to-end course revamp, from identifying a security learning gap to delivery and learner support.

6 completions · 2 weeks · 8 live training hours

Explore case study & screenshots

The learning gap

Learners were unsure which Salesforce security concept to use. I identified that the existing security module needed more detailed coverage and revised it to make those choices clearer.

My role

I owned learning needs analysis, curriculum design, material creation, training schedules, delivery, trainee support, and assessment. The course covered administration fundamentals, relationships, security and sharing, and Flow practice.

How I supported learners

I explained the three security layers and record access using visual slides and verbal explanations. I expanded the security module and connected concepts with structured assignments. A progressive workbook with 30 hands-on flows supported practice within the same course.

Delivery & tracking

Delivered eight one-hour sessions over two weeks, four days per week. I tracked attendance through Teams meeting reports and managed submissions and assessments through email, Google Forms, and the LMS.

Completion & observed improvements

Six trainees completed the revised course. I observed greater independence in security tasks, better assessment performance, and fewer repeated questions.

02Technical training

CMS Database: SQL Server

Project-based SQL learning supported by structured labs and facilitator resources.

7 learners · Training delivered

Explore case study & screenshots

Audience & learning need

QA engineers with basic SQL, applying database skills to a content management system with roles, permissions, and version history.

My role

I created learning materials and delivered the SQL/CMS training to seven learners.

Learning design

The learning sequence begins with a business brief and ERD design, then progresses through schema creation, constraints, queries, procedures, and database testing. Learner materials and trainer answers support guided practice and review.

Delivery resources

The package includes a learner guide, lab workbook, trainer answer key, preparation checklist, session guidance, and a 100-mark capstone rubric.
